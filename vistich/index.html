Good to know

A few little answers.

✳︎
What is Vistich?

Vistich is a photo collage app for iPhone. It helps you turn photos into editable collages, journals, and continuous carousel posts.

Is Vistich available on the App Store?

Not yet. Vistich is still in development, and its official App Store link and final store details will be added here when it is released.

Are my photos or projects uploaded?

No. Selected photos, editable projects, and rendered collages stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process usage and diagnostics, and RevenueCat processes subscription records; these services do not receive your photos or project content.

Does Vistich need an internet connection?

Yes. The planned first release requires an internet connection, including for its online services and subscription checks. No Vistich account is required.

Does Vistich require a subscription?

Premium features will be available through auto-renewable Apple App Store subscriptions managed with RevenueCat. Plans, included features, prices, and billing periods are shown before purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Can I create collages on this website?

This website introduces Vistich and its creative possibilities. Photo editing and collage creation take place in the iPhone app.
