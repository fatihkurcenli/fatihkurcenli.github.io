About This App

Vistich is a creative tool for editable photo collages, single posts, stories, and continuous carousel cards. Its planned initial App Store release requires an internet connection and can be used without a Vistich account. Premium features are offered through auto-renewable App Store subscriptions managed with RevenueCat. Photos and projects stay on your device; Vistich is not a cloud-storage or backup service.

You retain rights to your own photos and exported collages. You are responsible for having the rights and permissions needed for every photo, name, caption, sticker, or other item you import, create, save, publish, or share, and for keeping independent backups of important projects and exports.
