Easy PDF Scanner

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Scope and Publisher

This policy applies to Easy PDF Scanner, published by AutumnSun. It covers information handled when you install, open, or use a current version of the app. Easy PDF Scanner does not require a AutumnSun account and is not designed to collect your precise location. We do not sell personal data.

What the App Collects

Google Play currently states that Easy PDF Scanner does not collect data and does not share data with third parties.

Photos, scans, document names, and generated PDF files are processed to create the documents you request. They stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Permissions and Your Choices

Camera access is used to capture pages. Selected photo, media, and file access may be used to import images and save, open, or share PDF documents.

You can decline or later revoke optional permissions in your device's settings. The related feature may then be unavailable, while the rest of the app can continue to work where technically possible.

Analytics and Diagnostics

Easy PDF Scanner currently reports that it does not collect or share user data. It may still rely on Google Play services for distribution and updates.

Support Communications

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and files you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, protect the app, and keep necessary support records. Please do not send sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Sharing and Legal Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, disclosed when required by law, or used when reasonably necessary to respond to legal requests, protect users and rights, prevent fraud or abuse, and maintain app security and reliability.

Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local scans and PDFs remain until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Copies you export or share must be removed from their destination separately.

Third-party providers keep information according to their own policies. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, or record-keeping purposes. To ask about information we can access, contact us.

Children and Security

Easy PDF Scanner is intended for general audiences and does not ask children to create an account or submit names or contact details. Users below the age at which they can consent to data processing should use the app with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information we handle. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
