Frequently Asked Questions

Are my camera frames, photos, or videos uploaded?

No. Live frames and visual effects are processed on-device. Firebase receives limited product-interaction and diagnostic data, not the visual content of your media.

Does NostaLens read my photo library?

No. It requests add-only Photos access so it can save what you create without reading or editing the rest of your library.

Does NostaLens include purchases or subscriptions?

No. The current App Store version is free and has no paid features or subscriptions.
