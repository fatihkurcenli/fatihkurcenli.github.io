NostaLens - Vintage Camera

Shoot everyday moments with the warmth and character of vintage film.

NostaLens combines 23 crafted camera looks, live film previews, retro video, custom Layers, and a personal Roll in one focused iPhone and iPad camera. Visual processing stays on your device.

Highlights

  • Analogue-style photos and retro video with optional sound
  • Custom camera looks built and saved with adjustable Layers
  • 3:4, 4:5, and 1:1 framing with grid, level, timer, and date stamp
  • A local Roll for reviewing, organizing, saving, and sharing captures
  • On-device visual processing with no camera-frame uploads
