NostaLens - Vintage Camera

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Scope and Publisher

This policy applies to NostaLens - Vintage Camera, published by AutumnSun. It covers information handled when you install, open, or use a current version of the app. NostaLens - Vintage Camera does not require a AutumnSun account and is not designed to collect your precise location. We do not sell personal data.

What the App Collects

Apple's App Privacy label states that NostaLens may collect product interaction, crash data, and other diagnostic data for analytics and app functionality. This data is not linked to your identity. NostaLens has no account system, does not sell personal data, and does not use this technical data to track you across apps or websites.

Live camera frames, photographs, and videos are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers or analyzed by us for their visual content. Media in NostaLens Roll remains local unless you choose to save or share it, and the app does not perform facial recognition, extract biometric data, or read precise location or contacts.

Permissions and Your Choices

NostaLens requests iOS permissions only when a related capture or save feature needs them. You can review or change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera

    Used for the live viewfinder and to capture photographs or retro video with the selected camera look.

    The live viewfinder and in-app photo or video capture will not be available.

  • Add to Photos

    Used only to save photographs and videos you create to your photo library. NostaLens does not use this permission to read or edit the rest of your library.

    Your captures can remain in NostaLens Roll, but saving a copy to the system photo library will not be available.

  • Microphone

    Used only when you choose to record retro video with sound.

    You can still take photographs and record video without sound.

You can decline or later revoke optional permissions in your device's settings. The related feature may then be unavailable, while the rest of the app can continue to work where technically possible.

Analytics and Diagnostics

NostaLens uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand product interactions and diagnose reliability issues. These services may process app events, device and operating-system information, app configuration, crash data, other diagnostics, and an IP address used during transmission. This technical data is not used to upload or inspect your camera frames, photographs, or videos.

Relevant provider policies:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (opens in a new tab)

Support Communications

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and files you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, protect the app, and keep necessary support records. Please do not send sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Sharing and Legal Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, disclosed when required by law, or used when reasonably necessary to respond to legal requests, protect users and rights, prevent fraud or abuse, and maintain app security and reliability.

Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Photographs and videos in NostaLens Roll remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app. Copies saved or shared to another destination must be removed there separately. Firebase retains usage and diagnostic information according to its own policies.

Third-party providers keep information according to their own policies. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, or record-keeping purposes. To ask about information we can access, contact us.

Children and Security

NostaLens - Vintage Camera is intended for general audiences and does not ask children to create an account or submit names or contact details. Users below the age at which they can consent to data processing should use the app with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information we handle. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
