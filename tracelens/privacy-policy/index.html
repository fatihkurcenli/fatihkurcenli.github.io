Scope and Publisher

This policy applies to ARtist, published by AutumnSun. It covers information handled when you install, open, or use a current version of the app. ARtist does not require a AutumnSun account and is not designed to collect your precise location. We do not sell personal data.

What the App Collects

Google Play currently states that TraceLens does not share data with third parties. Its Data safety label says it may collect app activity, including in-app search history; app information and performance, including diagnostics and other performance data; and device or other identifiers for app functionality, analytics, and developer communications.

Images you choose and camera input are used to provide tracing and drawing guidance. Selected images and saved artwork remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them to another destination.

Permissions and Your Choices

Camera and selected photo or media access are requested only when needed for a feature you choose. Network access may support ads, updates, diagnostics, and Google Play services.

You can decline or later revoke optional permissions in your device's settings. The related feature may then be unavailable, while the rest of the app can continue to work where technically possible.

Analytics and Diagnostics

TraceLens may use Google services for distribution, advertising, analytics, diagnostics, crash reporting, and reliability, including ads shown through Google AdMob.

Support Communications

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and files you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, protect the app, and keep necessary support records. Please do not send sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Sharing and Legal Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, disclosed when required by law, or used when reasonably necessary to respond to legal requests, protect users and rights, prevent fraud or abuse, and maintain app security and reliability.

Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local images and artwork can be removed in the app where available, by clearing app storage, or by uninstalling. Google Play currently states that the disclosed technical data is not available for deletion through an app or account request.

Third-party providers keep information according to their own policies. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, or record-keeping purposes. To ask about information we can access, contact us.

Children and Security

ARtist is intended for general audiences and does not ask children to create an account or submit names or contact details. Users below the age at which they can consent to data processing should use the app with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information we handle. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
